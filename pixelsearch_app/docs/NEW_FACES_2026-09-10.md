## Delivered

- Search shows a People badge and a "New faces found" notice when indexed photos have faces awaiting a name. The notice opens People directly on its New faces view.
- New faces includes unassigned detections and existing unnamed groups. Naming an existing group updates that group in place; it does not create an extra person.
- The assignment sheet shows face previews, original-photo access, a new-person action, and searchable existing names/nicknames. Matching people move ahead of other actions while typing. Incorrect detections can still be dismissed.
- Named people has a searchable directory, face previews, photo counts, and the existing detail/edit/correction tools. Hidden and dismissed observations do not generate a new-face notice. Empty groups with a saved name remain accessible.
- Names are required in the naming dialog. "This is me" saves the local aliases `me` and `myself`. Duplicate names or aliases still require the existing explicit person choice in search.
- Saving a name refreshes an already-open search. Name-only search uses the confirmed photo assignments; name-plus-scene search constrains eligible photos before semantic ranking.
- `me with my friend <friend>` resolves both saved people and requires both in each photo. `me with my friend <friend> at sunset` passes `sunset` to the scene search with the same two-person constraint.
- `show me photos of <friend>` does not incorrectly require the person with the `me` alias. Relationship words are removed only immediately before a matched saved name; they do not establish a relationship record.
